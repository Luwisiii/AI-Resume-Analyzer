Privacy Policy

Last updated September 23, 2026

Resume Analyzer is a free portfolio project that scores a resume and matches it to public job postings. This page explains what it stores and who else sees it.

What we store

  • Your account: username, email address, and a hashed password. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we store that provider's account ID and the verified email it gives us, and no password. We never see or store your Google or GitHub password.
  • Resumes you upload: the PDF, the text extracted from it, the skills and feedback generated from it, and a numeric summary (an embedding) used for job matching.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in, and a CSRF cookie that protects your account from forged requests. No advertising or analytics cookies.

Who else processes it

  • Groq runs the AI model: resume text is sent to it to generate skills and feedback.
  • Render hosts the app and Neon hosts the database.
  • Google and GitHub, only if you choose to sign in with them.

Your resume is visible only to your account. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept until you ask for it to be deleted. Uploaded PDF files may be cleared sooner when the server restarts; the analysis stays. To delete your account and everything linked to it, email paoskidumayas09@gmail.com from the address on your account.
